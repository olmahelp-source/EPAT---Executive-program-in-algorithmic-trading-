## 515. Portfolio and margin management

**Formulas**
```
margin = sum of notional x margin rate
excess liquidity = equity - margin
under stress: equity falls by the P&L, notionals move, margin rates rise
share of the book to keep (s) to restore a buffer of b x equity:   s = (1 - b) x equity / (stressed margin - stress P&L)
```

**Worked example.** Equity 2,000,000. Index futures long 4.0M (6% margin), equities long 3.0M (25%), equities short 1.5M (30%).
- margin now **1,440,000**, excess liquidity **560,000**
- stress: market -10% (longs lose, shorts gain) and margin rates x1.5
  - P&L **-550,000**, equity falls to **1,450,000**, margin rises to **1,944,000**
  - excess liquidity **-494,000**: a margin call
- to restore a 10% buffer you must keep **72.2%** of the book, i.e. cut **27.8%**

**Code:** `t515_margin_projection`
**Watch out:** the cut is larger than the loss itself, because both equity and margin requirements get worse at once.

## 516. SPAN and exchange margin methodology

**Idea.** SPAN-style margin is the worst loss over a set of scenarios: price moves of 0, 1/3, 2/3, and the full scan range (up and down), plus extreme moves (3x the range) counted at 35%.

**Worked example.** One index future (5,000 x $50 = 250,000 notional), scan range 7%.
- one contract long: worst scenario is the extreme move, 3 x 7% x 250,000 x 35% = **18,375** (the plain 7% scan gives 17,500)
- one contract short: **18,375**
- long the front contract and short the back contract, with the back contract moving 0.97 of the front's move: **551**
- sum of the two standalone margins: 36,750, so the offset credit is **98.5%**

Real SPAN adds minimum spread charges, volatility scenarios for options, and exchange-set parameters. This captures the mechanism, not the exact numbers.

**Code:** `t516_span` (`span_like`)
**Watch out:** exchanges change scan ranges quickly in volatile markets, so your margin can double with no change in your positions.

## 517. Cross-margining and intraday margin

**Formulas**
```
standalone margin = z x (sigma_A x |V_A| + sigma_B x |V_B|)
cross margin     = z x sqrt( (sigma_A V_A)^2 + (sigma_B V_B)^2 + 2 rho (sigma_A V_A)(sigma_B V_B) )       (signed V)
```

**Worked example.** Long 1,000,000 of A (1.2% daily vol), short 900,000 of B (1.1%), correlation 0.9, z = 2.326.
- standalone margins add to **50,947**
- cross-margined: **12,347**, a **75.8% reduction**

Intraday margin (illustrative: 2.5% intraday against 10% overnight): with 100,000 of equity you can carry **4.0M** intraday but only **1.0M** overnight. Holding the intraday maximum through the cut-off forces you to cut **75%** of the position.

**Code:** `t517_cross_margin`
**Watch out:** a hedge only reduces margin while the correlation holds. If the correlation drops, the credit goes with it.

## 518. Broker insolvency and custody risk

**Formulas**
```
expected loss = probability of broker failure x loss given failure x (balance - protection)
```

**Worked example.** 10M at one broker, annual failure probability 0.5%.

| Arrangement | Expected annual loss | Loss if the broker fails |
|---|---|---|
| Segregated client assets (loss mainly delay costs, 2%) | 1,000 | **200,000** |
| Unsegregated or rehypothecated, 500k protection scheme (loss 40%) | 19,000 | **3,800,000** |
| Unsegregated, no protection scheme (loss 40%) | 20,000 | 4,000,000 |

Segregation reduces the failure loss by about 95%. The protection scheme covers only a small slice.

**Code:** `t518_custody`
**Watch out:** the assumed loss rates are illustrative. Read the broker's actual custody terms and the scheme's limits.

## 519. Funding-liquidity risk and liquidity-adjusted VaR

**Formulas**
```
liquidation days = ceil( position / (participation x ADV value) )
risk over the liquidation path = z x sigma x sqrt( sum of (exposure at the start of each day)^2 )
LVaR = path risk + spread cost + impact cost,   impact = c x sigma x sqrt(position / ADV) x position
```

**Worked example.** Position 10M, daily vol 1.5%, ADV 20M, participation 10%.
- 1-day 99% VaR: **348,952**
- days to liquidate: **5** (exposure 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 million at the start of each day)
- risk over the liquidation path: **517,580**
- spread cost 2,500 and impact cost (c = 0.5) **53,033**
- **LVaR 573,113, which is 1.64 times the plain VaR**

**Code:** `t519_liquidity_adjusted_var`
**Watch out:** a plain one-day VaR assumes you can exit tomorrow at the mid price.
